## API Reference
- [Visits - Total (Desktop & Mobile)](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/visits.md): Returns the estimated number of all visits to a given domain: desktop and mobile visits combined. Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Pages Per Visit - Total (Desktop & Mobile)](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/pages-per-visit.md): Returns the average number of page views per visit for a given domain. Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Average Visit Duration - Total (Desktop & Mobile)](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/average-visit-duration-all-traffic.md): Returns the average visit duration for a given domain (in seconds). Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Bounce Rate - Total (Desktop & Mobile)](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/bounce-rate.md): Returns the bounce rate for a given domain. Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Page Views - Total (Desktop & Mobile)](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/page-views.md): Returns the estimated number of page views for a domain. Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Desktop vs. Mobile Split - (Desktop & Mobile)](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/desktop-vs-mobile-split.md): Returns the desktop vs. mobile web traffic share split for the selected time period (1 month minimum).
- [Deduplicated Audience - (Desktop & Mobile)](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/deduplicated-audience.md): Returns the number of individuals visiting a domain across devices monthly (deduplicated across devices).
- [Global Rank - Total (Desktop & Mobile)](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/global-rank.md): Returns Similarweb's Global Rank for a given domain. Global Rank is calculated based on a mix of our estimations of a site's monthly unique visitors and monthly pageviews across desktop and mobile traffic.
- [Country Rank - Total (Desktop & Mobile)](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/country-rank.md): Returns Similarweb's monthly Country Rank for a given domain.
- [Industry Rank - Total (Desktop & Mobile)](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/industry-rank.md): Returns the industry (formerly named 'category') of a domain and its Industry Rank within its industry worldwide.
- [Geography - Total (Desktop & Mobile)](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/geography-total.md): Returns the total traffic share, visits, pages per visit, average time, bounce rate and rank broken down by country, between the selected dates (min. 1 month).
- [Visits - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/visits-desktop.md): Returns the estimated number of desktop visits to a domain. Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Pages Per Visit - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/pages-per-visit-desktop.md): Returns the average page views per desktop visit for a given domain. Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Average Visit Duration - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/average-visit-duration.md): Returns the average desktop visit duration on a given domain (in seconds). Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Bounce Rate - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/bounce-rate-desktop.md): Returns the bounce rate on desktop devices for a given domain. Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Pageviews - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/pageviews.md): Returns the estimated number of pageviews for a domain on desktop devices. Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Unique Visitors - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/unique-visitors.md): Returns the daily or monthly unique visitors to a domain on desktop devices. Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Geography - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/geography.md): Returns the desktop traffic share, visits, pages per visit, average time, bounce rate and rank broken down by country, between the selected dates (min. 1 month).
- [Visits - Mobile Web](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/visits-2.md): Returns the estimated number of mobile web visits to a domain. Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Pages Per Visit - Mobile Web](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/pages-per-visit-1.md): Returns the average pageviews per visit for a given domain, on mobile web. Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Average Visit Duration - Mobile Web](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/average-visit-duration-1.md): Returns the average mobile web visit duration on a given domain (in seconds). Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Bounce Rate - Mobile Web](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/bounce-rate-2.md): Returns the mobile web bounce rate for a given domain. Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Pageviews - Mobile Web](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/pageviews-1.md): Returns the mobile web page views for a given domain. Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Unique Visitors - Mobile Web](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/unique-visitors-1.md): Returns the daily or monthly unique visitors to a domain on mobile web. Historical data goes back 37 months, depending on your subscription.
- [Geography - Mobile](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/geography-mobile.md): Returns the mobile traffic share, visits, pages per visit, average time, bounce rate and rank broken down by country, between the selected dates (min. 1 month).
- [Visits by Marketing Channel - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/traffic-sources-overview.md): Returns the estimated desktop visits by marketing channel (Organic Search, Paid Search, Direct, Display Ads, E-Mail, Referrals and Social) on desktop devices.
- [Pages Per Visit by Marketing Channel - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/pages-per-visit-desktop-traffic-sources.md): Returns the estimated pages per visit by marketing channel (Organic Search, Paid Search, Direct, Display Ads, E-Mail, Referrals and Social) on desktop devices.
- [Average Visit Duration by Marketing Channel - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/average-visit-duration-desktop-traffic-sources.md): Returns the estimated average visit duration by marketing channel (Organic Search, Paid Search, Direct, Display Ads, E-Mail, Referrals and Social) on desktop devices.
- [Bounce Rate by Marketing Channel - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/bounce-rate-desktop-traffic-channels.md): Returns the estimated bounce rate by marketing channel (Organic Search, Paid Search, Direct, Display Ads, E-Mail, Referrals and Social) on desktop devices.
- [Referrals - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/referrals-desktop.md): Returns the referring websites for a given domain, traffic share, change per referrer, and # of total referral visits.
- [Social Referrals - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/social-referrals.md): Returns the leading social networks sending traffic to a domain as well as traffic share per social network and number of social visits.
- [Ad Networks - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/ad-networks-desktop.md): Returns the top Ad Networks sending traffic to a domain, as well as the traffic share per Ad Network.
- [Display Publishers - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/display-publishers-desktop.md): Returns the top publishers sending traffic to a given domain and traffic share per publisher.
- [Publishers Per Ad Network - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/publishers-per-ad-network-desktop.md): Returns the top publishers per ad network for a given domain, and their traffic share.
- [Organic Keyword Competitors - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/organic-keyword-competitors-desktop.md): Returns a list of websites that are competing for the same organic keywords as the given domain, overlap score and % of shared keywords.
- [Paid Keyword Competitors - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/paid-keyword-competitors-desktop.md): Returns a list of websites that are competing for the same paid keywords as the given domain, overlap score and % of shared keywords.
- [Organic Outgoing Links - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/organic-outgoing-links-desktop.md): This endpoint enables you to examine outgoing links from any website by returning the traffic share of all organic outgoing links for a domain and the number of outgoing visits.
- [Outgoing Ads: Ad Networks - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/outgoing-ads-ad-networks-desktop.md): Returns the traffic share of all Ad Networks receiving traffic from a domain.
- [Outgoing Ads: Advertisers - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/outgoing-ads-advertisers-desktop.md): Returns the traffic share of all advertisers receiving traffic from a domain.
- [Traffic Sources by Marketing Channel - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/desktop-marketing-channels-1.md): Returns a list of desktop website traffic sources (domain) by marketing channel (source type) and traffic share for the selected time frame.
- [PPC Spend - Desktop](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/ppc-spend-desktop.md): Returns an estimate of a website’s spend on PPC search terms over an analyzed period.
- [Traffic Sources by Marketing Channel - Mobile Web](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/traffic-sources-overview-mobile-web.md): Returns the estimated mobile visits by channel: Organic Search, Paid Search, Direct, Display Ads, E-Mail, Referrals and Social.
- [Referrals - Mobile Web](https://developers.similarweb.com/reference/referrals-mobile-web.md): Returns the referring websites for a given domain on mobile web, traffic share per referrer, and change, as well as the number of incoming mobile visits.
